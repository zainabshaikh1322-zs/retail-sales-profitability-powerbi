Business Insights and Recommendations
This document summarizes the initial findings from my Retail Sales &
Profitability Power BI dashboard and identifies follow-up analyses that
could support better business decisions.
Note: These are descriptive observations from the current
dashboard, not proof of why a result occurred. Figures are approximate
and should be checked with all slicers reset to All before
publication.

1. Overall Sales and Profitability
Finding: The dashboard displays approximately 2.33M in sales,
292.30K in profit, and a 12.56% profit margin.

Business meaning: The business is profitable overall in the current
view, but an overall margin can hide products, regions, or orders that
perform poorly.

Recommendation: - Monitor sales, profit, and margin together. -
Compare profit margin across categories, regions, and customer
segments. - Calculate the total profit impact of loss-making orders
before deciding where to focus.
2. Product Category Performance
Finding: Technology has the highest sales among the three displayed
categories.

Business meaning: Technology is a major sales contributor, but the
highest-selling category is not necessarily the most profitable.

Recommendation: - Compare profit and margin across Technology,
Furniture, and Office Supplies. - Identify products with high sales but
relatively low profit. - Review discount levels and product mix before
recommending promotions.
3. Regional Sales Performance
Finding: The dashboard shows the West and East as the largest
regional contributors to sales. Approximate values displayed are:

  Region        Sales   Share of sales
  West        739.81K            31.8%
  East        691.83K            29.7%
  Central     503.17K            21.6%
  South       391.72K            16.8%

Business meaning: West and East account for the largest shares of
sales in the current view. The South has the smallest share, but lower
sales alone do not prove poor performance.

Recommendation: - Compare regional profit and profit margin, not
just sales. - Investigate product mix, customer mix, and discounts by
region. - Explore reasons for the South's lower sales using available
data before suggesting changes.
4. Customer Segment Performance
Finding: Consumer customers contribute approximately 1.17M, or
50.32%, of sales. Corporate contributes around 30.77%, and Home Office
around 18.92%.

Business meaning: Consumer is the largest sales segment, but sales
share alone does not show which segment is most profitable.

Recommendation: - Compare profit, margin, order count, and average
sales per order across segments. - Identify which products perform well
within each segment. - Evaluate promotions based on their effect on
profit as well as sales.
5. Sub-Categories with Negative Profit
Finding: Tables and Bookcases appear to have negative total profit
in the current sub-category profit chart.

Business meaning: These areas may be reducing overall profitability,
but the chart does not reveal the cause.

Recommendation: - Review product- and order-level sales, profit,
quantity, and discounts. - Investigate shipping costs or other cost
fields if available. - Check whether losses are concentrated in a few
orders or occur broadly. - Consider pricing, discount, or product-mix
changes only after investigating the drivers.
6. Sales Trend Over Time
Finding: The yearly sales chart shows an upward pattern across the
displayed years.

Business meaning: Sales appear to rise over the period shown.
However, the latest year may be incomplete, which can make comparisons
misleading.

Recommendation: - Check the date coverage for each year. - Compare
equivalent periods across years. - Review profit and margin alongside
sales growth. - Use monthly or quarterly trends to investigate unusual
changes if the data supports it.
7. Loss-Making Orders
Finding: The dashboard displays approximately 1K loss-making orders.
The measure counts distinct orders where the combined profit across
their rows is negative.

Business meaning: This identifies orders worth investigating, but
the count alone does not show the financial impact.

Recommendation: - Calculate loss-making orders as a percentage of
all distinct orders. - Calculate the combined profit or loss associated
with these orders. - Break them down by product sub-category, region,
customer segment, discount, and ship mode. - Prioritize the largest
losses rather than focusing only on the number of affected orders.
Recommended Next Steps
1. Compare profit margins across categories and regions.
2. Investigate loss-making sub-categories and orders.
3. Check whether discount levels are associated with lower profit.
4. Confirm whether the latest year is complete before describing
   year-on-year growth.
5. Validate all KPI figures, currency units, and date filters before
   publishing.
Conclusion
The dashboard provides an initial view of retail sales and
profitability. The current observations highlight regional sales
concentration, the importance of the Consumer segment, possible
sub-category losses, and orders with negative combined profit.

The recommendations are investigation priorities, not proven causal
conclusions. Further analysis is needed before changing pricing,
discounting, promotions, or product strategy.
